# Tickersoft

**The "Everything WebApp" & Social Hub for Legacy Java & Modern Devices Alike**

**Official Website:** <https://tickersoft.de>
(Available dynamically via `http://tickersoft.de` for legacy WAP/XHTML browsers)

Welcome to the official GitHub presence for **Tickersoft**.

Tickersoft is an ultra-lightweight, zero-tracker web suite and social platform designed to breathe new life into vintage mobile devices, while streamlining the more modern ones. Engineered to run straight from any stock WAP browser, it strips away the bloat of the modern internet to deliver pure, uninterrupted functionality.

Tickersoft has been proven to load in SECONDS over connection speeds as low as 18KB/s (!), making it far more reliable in terms of speed, especially for the more rural areas, or those of us who can rely on us as a fallback as soon as their data bundles are sucked dry, because your short form content farms won't load.

**But we will.**

**Our Core Mission:** To provide minimum compatibility for maximum utility. We specifically cater to J2ME/Java clients for users whose stock WAP browsers are too janky or incapable of properly running third-party alternatives, while simultaneously offering a lightning-fast, distraction-free lifeline for any modern device on a throttled connection.

## What is Tickersoft.de?

Tickersoft proves that the web does not need megabytes of JavaScript or corporate trackers to be useful. Whether you are using a classic Nokia, a vintage BlackBerry, a Java-enabled device, a computer (old or new) or a modern smartphone on a 2G-speed throttled data plan, Tickersoft brings essential online tools back to your fingertips. It is the true "Everything WebApp" built for maximum resilience.

While our main focus is on J2ME and WAP phones, most (if not all) mobile devices or computers from as early as 2005 are fully supported for accessing Tickersoft and our affiliated websites. We have not created any dedicated desktop programs yet, but the web portal is open to everyone.

Available in 4 languages: **English, German, Dutch, and Polish**.

## Frictionless & Secure Access

We want to make logging into vintage devices as easy as possible, but without sacrificing an ounce of security. Tickersoft features a custom TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) engine.

Unlike modern authenticator apps that track your habits, our TOTP engine is completely custom, zero-tracking, and fully offline. It is specifically designed to let you log in securely on the exact same device you are using to browse. We also support email codes as a lightweight login method, with more features planned to streamline the authentication process for older hardware.

## Dedicated J2ME Java Clients

To mitigate browser limitations on older hardware, we are actively developing a suite of native J2ME (Java ME) applications. These clients bring advanced, modern functionality directly to your vintage phone:

* **JavaChat (IT IS ALIVE!):** A functional MIDlet that lets you use Tickersoft DMs, Kchan reloaded (our help forums), and the public IRC completely natively. It actually works now!

* **Tickersoft TOTP Client (ALPHA):** Generate offline codes and log into Tickersoft safely directly on the same device. You can configure this easily in your Tickerkonto Policy.

* **TickerMail (PRE-ALPHA):** A dedicated, native J2ME IMAP/SMTP email client designed specifically for older hardware.

* **MauMau (PRE-ALPHA):** Kick back with some casino-style multiplayer Crazy 8s (MauMau) right on your vintage mobile.

## A Healthier Digital Goal: The Philosophy

The classic aesthetic is cool, but Tickersoft aims for much more than that. Our goal is to make these devices capable of handling daily essentials, becoming a lifeline against hyperconnected exhaustion. We offer the useful parts of the smartphone boom without the toxicity.

Constant video and streaming mean we are always visible, fueling parasocial illusions and addictive habits that age restrictions struggle to fix. By returning to intentional connection, sharing text and photos without endless video feeds, we offer a quieter, healthier alternative.

Tickersoft was built as a direct protest **AGAINST** the modern web's obsession with data harvesting, massive payloads, and making a quick buck off user privacy. We do not log your searches. We do not use Big Tech trackers.

This is our attempt at the retro dream. We want classic devices made as capable as we originally hoped they would be. With our servers doing the heavy lifting, we hope to slowly contribute to a societal shift over the next 5 to 10 years, building a more sustainable relationship with technology.
